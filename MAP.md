# EMMAUS — The Unfolding
### A Scripture-discovery journey game

> "And beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, he explained to them what was said
> in all the Scriptures concerning himself." — Luke 24:27

**This document is the single source of truth for the project. If either of us forgets a
decision, we come back here.**

---

## 1. What this is

One game. Not ten. A single core mechanic — **the same verse, at increasing depth** —
repeated across **ten chapters**. The player walks a journey from the Garden to the City,
and each chapter is *also* a real area of life they are trying to overcome.

The goal is not points. The goal is that a person **never reads a verse the same way again**,
and that the **salvation journey is uncovered** to them as they go.

---

## 2. The core mechanic — The Unfolding Lens

The player holds a "lens" over a verse. Each level of zoom reveals one layer.
Deeper layers give more **Marks**.

| Layer | Name | What opens up |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Plain** | What the verse says |
| 2 | **The Words** | Hebrew / Greek meaning, shown beside the translations |
| 3 | **The Thread** | The hidden type of Christ in the Old Testament → its New Testament fulfilment |
| 4 | **The Practice** | A real-life situation + the biblical way through it |
| 5 | **The Watch** | How to pray it — including the deeper prayer truths (Daniel's 21 days, praying in the Spirit, the night watch) |

**Marks** rise with *depth*, not repetition. Layer 1 gives a little. All five makes the
chapter "glow" and marks it complete.

---

## 3. Truth tags (this is what makes the game trustworthy)

Every reveal carries one of three small badges. This is non-negotiable — it is what will
make teachers *recommend* the game instead of warning about it.

| Tag | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Text says** | The Bible states this plainly or the NT explicitly confirms it | Manna → "I am the bread of life" (John 6:35) |
| 🟡 **Tradition holds** | A sincere reading held by a Christian tradition, not stated outright | "Praying in the Spirit" as tongues (1 Cor 14:14) |
| 🔵 **A reading** | A devotional or allegorical reading some see; beautiful, but not asserted by the text | "Shekinah" (a later rabbinic term — *not in the Bible*) |

**Rule:** never blur these. Honesty is the feature.

## 4. The ten chapters — the journey map

Each chapter = one **life area** + one stage of the **salvation story** + one **type of Christ**.

| # | Chapter | Life area it heals | Type of Christ (OT → NT) | Word study | Prayer focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Garden** | Identity — "who am I?" | Adam → the Last Adam (Rom 5:14; 1 Cor 15:45) | *tselem* (image), *nephesh* (living being) | Adoration & thanksgiving |
| 2 | **The Fall** | Shame, guilt, failure | The coats of skin (Gen 3:21) → our covering | *chata* (sin = "miss the mark"), *kaphar* (to cover) | Confession |
| 3 | **The Ark** | Fear, crisis, sudden loss | The ark & the door in its side → the open side of Christ | *tebah* (ark) | Surrender & petition |
| 4 | **The Lamb** | Guilt, need for cleansing | Passover lamb → "Behold, the Lamb of God" (John 1:29; 1 Cor 5:7) | *pesach* (passover), *seh* (lamb) | Thanksgiving for the cross |
| 5 | **The Wilderness** | Worry, provision, waiting | Manna → Bread of Life (John 6); the Rock → 1 Cor 10:4 | *manna* ("what is it?") | Daily bread & trust |
| 6 | **The Temple** | Emptiness, longing for God | Tabernacle → His body (John 2:19–21); we are the temple (1 Cor 6:19) | *shachah* (to bow down) | Worship in spirit & truth |
| 7 | **The Veil** | Rejection, feeling cut off from God | The torn veil (Matt 27:51) → bold access (Heb 10:19–20) | *hilasterion* (propitiation / mercy seat) | Intercession |
| 8 | **The Tomb** | Hopelessness, defeat, addiction | Jonah's three days (Matt 12:40); firstfruits (1 Cor 15:20) | *anastasis* (resurrection) | Declaration & faith |
| 9 | **The Upper Room** | Spiritual battle, weakness, fear | Pentecost — the Spirit poured out (Acts 2) | *pneuma* (spirit), *glōssa* (tongue), *proseuchomai* (pray) | **Praying in the Spirit; the night watch** |
| 10 | **The City** | Purpose, legacy, hope | The Bride & the wedding supper → the Church (Rev 21–22) | *parousia* (coming), *maranatha* ("come, Lord") | "Come, Lord Jesus" |

**Chapter 9 carries the specific revelations** the vision named: Daniel's 21 days,
praying in tongues, true worshippers, and the night timing of spiritual events.
Placed deliberately — it is the chapter about spiritual power, right before the final mission.

## 5. Chapter 9 detail — the deep-prayer chapter (handled honestly)

This is the most sensitive chapter, so it gets its own rules.

| Claim | How the game presents it | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Daniel prayed 21 days and was then answered | The angel says *"from the first day... thy words were heard"* (Dan 10:12) — the delay was **opposition** ("the prince of Persia withstood me"), not payment. The game reveals this as a *"you may have been taught this differently"* moment. | 🟢 Text says |
| Praying in the Spirit = praying in tongues | 1 Cor 14:14–15 and Jude 20. Honest note: some traditions read "in the Spirit" as Spirit-led prayer generally. | 🟡 Tradition holds |
| "True worshippers worship in spirit and in truth" | John 4:23–24 is about the **nature** of worship (not place, not ritual). The game links it to praying in tongues but does **not** fuse the two into one statement. | 🟢 Text says |
| Angels and night timing | Real night events exist: midnight prayer (Acts 16:25), the angel at night (Acts 12), angels at night (Luke 2). The game shows the **pattern**; it does **not** sell a fixed angelic schedule. | 🟢 Text says / 🔵 A reading |

## 6. Manna — the daily engine

- One puzzle a day, **the same for everyone** (Wordle-style).
- **Cannot be stockpiled.** Yesterday's puzzle is gone. This is the lesson of Exodus 16:19–20
  ("some of them left of it until the morning, and it bred worms") made into a mechanic.
- Completing Manna earns **Marks**.
- **Marks unlock the next chapter.** So the loop is:
  *come daily → get manna → grow Marks → unlock a chapter → unfold deeper.*

---

## 7. Kids and adults — two lamps, one chapter

Not a difficulty slider. A discipleship structure.

- **Little Lamp (kids):** the story as visual drag-and-drop. One truth, one practice.
- **Deep Lamp (adults):** word studies, types, prayer truths.
- **Family Mode:** adult and child play the *same chapter together*. When the adult unfolds a
  deeper layer, a **light** appears the child can see — so the child *watches* the parent go
  deeper. Faith passes down by modelling.

---

## 8. The Pulpit — the sermon layer

Every chapter ends with a **Pulpit** tab that auto-builds a sermon outline from that
chapter's own exegesis:

> **Text → Words → Thread → Practice → Watch**

- **Preacher mode:** a full outline they can preach.
- **Small-group mode:** a group competes to build the most *faithful* outline.
  Scored on **fidelity to the text**, not cleverness.

The sermon is not separate content — it is the chapter's data rendered as an outline.

## 10. Other features

- **Multiple users:** more than one profile on the same device, each with their own Marks,
  chapters unlocked, and notebook.
- **Notebook:** save a verse, write what you learned in this chapter or text, come back later.
- **Community:** one-tap links to a WhatsApp group, a Telegram group, Facebook, and other
  channels, so players can ask each other questions and chart progress together.
- **Visual style:** warm, illustrated, journey-map feel — not a plain quiz.

---

## 11. Design rules (the things we must not break)

1. **Never sell the reward.** No "read 5 chapters to unlock a skin." That turns devotion into
   a transaction.
2. **Never guilt.** No "you broke your 40-day streak." Grace-based content cannot use shame
   as a retention loop.
3. **Let the text win.** The best session ends with the player *reading*, not with a score.
4. **Always tag the truth.** 🟢 / 🟡 / 🔵 on every reveal.
5. **Quote accurately.** Scripture text is fetched from public-domain sources and never
   invented.
